## Arrangement

### Lead instruments
- Accordion — main melody line and solo during instrumental break
- Brass (trumpet + trombone) — reinforcement on chorus and final chorus
- Electric guitar — rhythmic, supporting role

### Rhythm
- Strong kick drum on beats 1 and 3
- Snare on beats 2 and 4
- Crowd handclaps on beats 2 and 4 from pre-chorus onwards

### Section-by-section build
- **Intro:** accordion solo, light percussion
- **Verse:** accordion + guitar, understated
- **Pre-Chorus:** build with brass entry
- **Chorus:** full arrangement, everything in
- **Bridge:** stripped back — accordion + crowd vocals only
- **Instrumental break:** accordion solo, band light underneath
- **Final Chorus:** key modulation +1 semitone, maximum energy, brass dominant
- **Outro:** fade with crowd chant, simple accordion

| Chorus | "olé!" | Crowd shout |

---

## Key change

The final chorus modulates up +1 semitone (G major → Ab major). This gives the closing section an extra lift and is a classic schlager technique for maximum festival impact.

---

## Vocals

- **Lead:** male voice, warm and dramatic — slightly over the top for the schlager vibe
- **Backing vocals:** mixed choir on chorus and bridge
- **Double tracking:** lead vocal doubled on chorus for body and presence
- **Ad libs:** encouraged on outro and final chorus

## Notes

- Title as action command ("fass" = grab/hold) — visual and instantly singable
- Pre-chorus builds comedic tension before the chorus explodes
- Bridge kept deliberately minimal — la-la and da-da always work live
- Song title repeated at least 4 times per chorus round for maximum retention
