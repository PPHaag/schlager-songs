## Suno tips

- Use **Custom Mode** — paste style prompt and lyrics separately
- **Title:** Die Blauen Himmel
- The `[Intro - a capella, no instruments]` tag is critical — Suno needs the explicit instruction to hold back instruments
- If the beat drop after the intro is not strong enough, add `massive beat drop after a capella intro` to the style prompt
- The `[Break]` section with Moog bass is the hardest to get right — if Suno misses it, try generating a separate clip for the break and splice it in post
- The `[2 beats complete silence]` tag may not be respected automatically — flag this for manual post-production editing if needed
- The spoken word moment: add `dramatic spoken word interlude in break` to style prompt if Suno skips it
- Generate 3–4 versions — listen specifically for: (1) quality of beat drop after intro, (2) Moog break energy, (3) silence before final chorus
- Key modulation on final chorus is often not automatic in Suno — note as manual post-production step if absent
