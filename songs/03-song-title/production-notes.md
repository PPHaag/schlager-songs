## Arrangement

### Instruments
- Electric guitar — DJ Ötzi-style power chords, present throughout, dominant in Moog break solo
- Accordion — main melody in verses and choruses, drops out during Moog break
- Moog synthesizer bass — enters on break, thick and electronic, drives the climax section
- Brass (trumpet + trombone) — accent on chorus hits and final chorus
- Kick drum + snare — hard 4/4 throughout at 130 BPM
- Crowd handclaps — beats 2 and 4 from pre-chorus onwards

### Guitar tone
Reference: DJ Ötzi — "Hey Baby", "Anton aus Tirol"  
Characteristics: slightly distorted, full mid-range, festival-loud, not metal but unmistakably electric. Power chords on every downbeat. No subtlety.

### Section-by-section build

- **A capella intro:** voices only — lead + backing in call/response, crescendo to unison shout. No instruments. 4 bars.
- **Beat drop (after intro):** guitar, kick, accordion all hit simultaneously on beat 1. Immediate full energy.
- **Verse:** guitar + accordion + drums, lead vocal forward
- **Pre-Chorus:** backing vocals enter with echo lines — tension builds with brass
- **Chorus:** full arrangement — guitar, accordion, brass, rhythm section, full choir on lines 5–8
- **Moog break:** accordion OUT, Moog bass IN — thick, electronic, stomping. Guitar switches to dirty power chord riff. Backing chants escalate. Spoken word moment from lead. 8-bar guitar solo. Build to shout climax. Then: 2 beats of complete silence.
- **Bridge:** stripped back — accordion returns, crowd la-la, all voices unison
- **Final Chorus:** key modulation +1 semitone (A major → Bb major), everything on maximum, guitar prominent, backing choir full
- **Outro:** guitar stays in, accordion fades, backing vocals carry the fade

---

## Moog break — detail

The centrepiece of the track. Structured in three phases:

**Phase 1 — Chant escalation (8 bars)**  
Backing vocals chant "Blau — blau — blau — blau!" four times with increasing volume. Moog bass drives underneath. Crowd joins in.

**Phase 2 — Spoken word drop (2 bars)**  
Lead vocal speaks, intimate and dramatic:  
*"Schau' mal rauf — sieh' den Himmel — heute Nacht gehört er uns!"*  
Music continues underneath but pulls back slightly to let the voice through.

**Phase 3 — Guitar solo + shout climax (8 bars)**  
DJ Ötzi-style guitar riff — full festival energy. All voices join on "DIE BLAUEN HIMMEL — HEY!" three times, building to maximum.

**Silence moment:** after the final HEY — 2 full beats of complete silence. Then the final chorus hits hard.

---

## Key change

Final Chorus modulates up +1 semitone (A major → Bb major). The guitar riff shifts with it — this is the moment where everything feels like it lifts off the ground.

## Notes

- The a capella intro is the defining risk and reward of the track — no music for 4 bars creates maximum tension. The beat drop on bar 5 must be massive.
- Guitar tone is deliberately "wrong" — too loud, too midrange, too fun. That's the point.
- The 2-beat silence before the final chorus is the most important production decision in the song. Do not cut it short.
- Backing vocalists should feel like a second lead, not a background element — especially in the Moog break chant sequence.
- The spoken word moment in the break should feel cinematic and slightly absurd — it's schlager, lean into the drama.
